Pasta

Pasta is a staple food made from an unleavened dough of durum wheat semolina mixed with water or eggs, formed into various shapes and cooked by boiling.

Here is the standard step-by-step order of preparation for cooking dry noodles (like instant noodles, ramen, or wheat noodles):

  1. Boil the water: Fill a pot with enough water to fully submerge the noodles and bring it to a rolling boil over high heat.
  2. Add the noodles: Place the noodle block or strands into the boiling water.
  3. Stir and loosen: Gently stir the noodles after a minute to separate the strands so they cook evenly.
  4. Incorporate seasoning: Add your flavor packets, sauces, or spices. For instant soup noodles, add them directly to the boiling water; for dry/stir-fried noodles, hold the seasoning until after draining.
  5. Cook to texture: Let them boil for the package's recommended time (usually 2 to 5 minutes) until they are tender but still firm.
  6. Drain and serve: Pour out the excess water if making dry noodles, or transfer the noodles and broth directly into a bowl to enjoy.


Ingredient to Prepare Pasta

  • Flour: 2 cups of all-purpose flour (creates the structural base).
  • Eggs: 3 large eggs (provides richness, color, and binding).
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon (enhances the dough flavor).
  • Water or Milk: 1 to 2 tablespoons, used only if the dough feels too dry to come together.
